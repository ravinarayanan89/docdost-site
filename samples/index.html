Fictional documents for trying DocDost AI (and for App Review). Every name, number and company is made up, and each page is marked "SAMPLE DOCUMENT – FICTIONAL DATA – NOT VALID".

  1. Car insurance.pdf — Car insurance policy (Northwind, fictional)
  2. Passport.pdf — Passport (fictional)
  3. Train ticket to Chennai.pdf — Train ticket
  4. AC warranty.pdf — AC warranty card
  5. Health insurance.pdf — Health insurance policy
  6. Electricity bill.pdf — Electricity bill

How to try them

  1. On your iPhone, tap a file, then Share → Save to Files.
  2. In DocDost AI, tap + → Import files and pick it. Add one document at a time; the AI reads each one in under a minute.
  3. Then ask, for example: "What is my passport number?", "Is my AC still under warranty?", "How many days until my train to Chennai?", "Is flood damage covered on my car?"
