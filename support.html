DocDost AI support

DocDost AI · Privacy policy

Questions, problems or ideas? Email ravi0389@gmail.com. Please don't send copies of your documents; describe the problem instead.

Which iPhones are supported?

DocDost AI needs iOS 26. On-device answers and reading need an iPhone with 6 GB of memory (iPhone 15 or later). On other iPhones, your documents, search and reminders still work, without the AI.

Why does the app download 1.36 GB?

DocDost's AI runs on your iPhone, not in the cloud. The first time, it downloads two model files once (about 1.05 GB and 310 MB). Use Wi-Fi if you can. After that, it works offline.

Why can I add only one document at a time?

The AI reads each document on your iPhone, which takes a moment and uses the iPhone's processor. Adding one at a time keeps it quick and keeps your iPhone cool. Add the next one once it's done.

An answer or detail looks wrong

The AI can make mistakes. Check the original (each detail shows the page it came from), then tap the detail to correct it. Reminders use only the dates you've confirmed.

Where are my documents stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the app's protected storage. There is no account and no server. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Delete documents in the app (swipe left on a document), or delete the app to remove all of its storage from your iPhone.
