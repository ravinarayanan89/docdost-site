What it does

Everything important, in one place.

Add a document once. DocDost AI takes care of the rest, right on your iPhone.

It reads your documents for you

Add a PDF from Files, scan a paper or pick a photo. The on-device AI reads every page and finds the numbers and dates that matter, so you never type them in.

  • Policy numbers, IDs, amounts and dates
  • Each value checked against the page
  • Text recognition for scans and photos

Just ask

"What's my passport number?" "Is my AC still under warranty?" "Is flood damage covered on my car?" Get a clear answer from your own documents, with the page it came from.

  • Plain-language questions and follow-ups
  • Answers point to the exact document and page
  • Works offline after a one-time setup

Never miss a renewal

See what needs you at a glance: policies ending, warranties running out, bills due, journeys coming up. Reminders arrive before anything expires.

  • Countdowns for every important date
  • Reminders 60, 30, 15 and 7 days before
  • Grouped into life areas: vehicles, health, home, IDs

Every detail, checked by you

Review what the AI found in a simple table. Tap any value to correct it, add your own, and confirm. Reminders only use dates you've confirmed.

Find anything in seconds

Search names, numbers and words inside every document. Filter by life area and see each document's status at a glance.

How it works

Three steps. No sign-up.

1

Set up the private AI

A one-time download (about 1.36 GB) puts the AI on your iPhone. After that, it works offline.

2

Add your documents

Import from Files, scan with the camera or pick from Photos, one at a time. The AI reads each in under a minute.

3

Ask and get reminded

Ask anything about your documents, and get reminders before policies, warranties and IDs expire.

Privacy

Your documents never leave your iPhone.

DocDost AI runs entirely on your device. The only download is the AI itself, once.

No account, no cloud

No sign-up and no server. Reading, search and answers all happen on your iPhone.

No tracking, no ads

No analytics, no advertising and no data sold. Nothing you add or ask is sent anywhere.

Protected storage

Files stay in the app's private storage, protected by iOS Data Protection, with an optional Face ID lock.
